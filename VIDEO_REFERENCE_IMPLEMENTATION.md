# Video reference implementation

The supplied 13.4-second video was used as a visual reference for the About Us GeM Journey section only.

Implemented design cues:
- Large editorial section heading.
- Five-stage journey arranged as an alternating/zig-zag visual path.
- Floating isometric/3D-style platforms with a soft cyan/teal glow.
- Dotted vertical connectors and animated endpoint pulses.
- Hover tilt/lift interaction on each stage.
- Responsive single-column stacking on mobile/fold screens and five-column composition on large screens.

MA Creation content is used instead of the video's original subject/content:
1. Registration & Documents
2. Catalog & Listing
3. Tender & Bidding
4. Orders & Fulfilment
5. Reporting & Growth

The Journey and AI Advantage sections were removed from Home and moved to About Us.
